## 共用环境段

```text
Edit image 1, the existing Chinese river ferry playable map. Single native horizontal full-bleed 16:9 gameplay frame. Make an EMPTY ENVIRONMENT PLATE with absolutely ZERO HUMANS because four matching actors will be composited at their exact positions later. Inpaint the left teal kerchief worker under the awning, right straw hat man AND his hand-held vertical staff, and small distant white-robed figure on rear stairs OUT cleanly. Leave existing dock wooden L-shaped lantern fixture, hanging lantern, architecture, barrels, boats, exact camera and paths intact. Keep the exact architectural positions and original crop to allow exchanging shots, foreground water occupies bottom fifth, pale stone shore, center stairs, exact roofs, left canopy, right boat. Goal is art-directed HD-2D rendered gameplay captured through a miniature lens, not clinical sharpened stone. A distinct focus band at the lower quay and immediate props, soften distant gables / small rear windows and nearest river with an optical lens and highlight bokeh, gentle transition with depth, clear focused silhouette on middle stones; a little local halation on real highlights, smooth contrast rolloff, dark corners. Make stones and water an organized set of broad tonal shapes, less hypercrisp linework and texture noise and fewer bathroom-tile-like shiny cobbles. Image 2 is a lighting and lens reference only, keep the geometry of image 1 independent of that. Spiritual kin of Octopath Traveler II's canal and Wandering Sword's Chinese ferry. NO HUD, dialogue, title, text, borders, collage. Do not add foreground tree branches that cover the walking lane.
```

## 正午

```text
HIGH NOON with strong unmistakable TYNDALL / volumetric sunlight in moist air. This is the central request. The high summer sun is just outside the top of the frame to the left. A handsome broad fan of 2 or 3 parallel, steeply descending, SOFT beams breaks through gaps in the left willow canopy. These beams must be clearly visible at thumbnail size across a shaded background, with uneven occluded edges and a little dust in that local air. They lose visibility outside the humid willow shade. The intervening foliage actually blocks light, casts broad leaf shadows. Follow-through: the beams land on the centre-left lower quay and left-center stair treads in luminous pale straw-white pools; a scatter of bright aqua-silver reflected flecks under the near boat. Cool green half-shadow under that canopy, weighty deep shade under shelter and on stone risers. Steep light direction, nothing orange, warm white and muted jade midtones with a little honey in the strongest sunlight. Sunlit / shaded areas form LARGE distinguishable shapes in a thumbnail, retain highlight gradation. Lamps unlit. Selective softness on nearer water and far roofs. Read as precisely midday not amber morning.
```

## 黄昏

```text
Lock the ferry layout. DUSK while last sliver of late peach-amber sunlight enters from screen left, clearly lower and raker than noon; breaks over bark, crowns and one stone course, thin copper trim on the right boat bamboo. Soft mauve and plum fill in the quay shadow, the old ferry lantern lights up and just starts defining a localized little warm pool on the boarding plank; a few upper lattice windows lit. Violet and salmon reflections under the left hull, muted amber accents near right. Give the light a soft filmic shoulder as in the reference. Controlled vignette, soft far windows, hazy localized last sunbeam with long shore shadows; atmospheric depth in this fixed map, avoid pervasive sepia.
```

## 夜晚

```text
True full NIGHT, retain all 3 original humans removed, maintain camera. Indigo-teal distant rooftops, selectively defocused round-to-soft-polygon golden bokeh in a couple of rear windows, one matte mauve tint in far ambience. Existing hanging dock lantern is principal light. Warm core gently blooms, paints an inviting soft pool 3 sprite-widths across on pier, staircase risers, and gangplank. Dark sliver under every object, crispest details at the steps beside lantern. Break up the light in the river as a few dancing vertical amber strokes softly defocused in front. Resolve route and shapes in deep blue, no daylight rays or crowds. Signed contrast between velvet darks and amber illuminated wood, not a cobalt color wash.
```

## NPC 透明图

```text
Generate an actual transparent PNG single-row character spritesheet for the Chinese wuxia RPG in reference 1. Only three small full-length STANDING NPC sprites, equally spaced side-by-side, each a separate clean cutout with their feet on one invisible baseline and equal scale from head to boot. Empty transparent space between and around sprites. Use EXACTLY the same old-school low-resolution 48-pixel-tall sprite grammar, native proportions, 3/4 view, block eyes and face silhouette as this source asset. Magnify via nearest-neighbour for sheet output. Design the 3 like this character's original pixel artist placed new hair and clothes over the same base head, hands, feet; face consists of 2-3 coarse peach shapes with 1 pixel dark angled eye and pale temple, no eye sclera circles, no noses outlined, no detailed lips, no round plastic / disney faces. Broad clean flat clusters, near-black stair-step contours, each fold communicated by one or two pixels, 15–22 colors per sprite. First at LEFT: adult dock porter, head turned RIGHT, low tied teal cotton headscarf, short cream-grey work tunic, mahogany sash, dark pants and plain boots, arms resting naturally, empty hands. Second in CENTER: older lean boatman facing LEFT with broad angular woven straw hat, slate blue cross-collar knee-length work robe, exposed wrists, dark boots, one hand on a slender straight brown bamboo punting pole vertically on viewer's RIGHT of his body; pole extends four sprite pixels above hat, tapers down beside the feet. Third on RIGHT: young traveler facing LEFT, cropped blue-grey cloth cap, pale off-white long robe with muted blue trim, dark shoes; hands resting together. A single row of three different individual job costumes; head to body proportion strictly matches reference not chibi bobbleheads. Entire feet, entire pole, entire hat visible. No text, no sketches, no floor, no shadows, no backgrounds, no scenery, no cells/outlines, no hero. High quality game-ready pixel assets.
```
